Privacy Notice.

This notice explains how Bespoke Aviation Limited handles personal information provided through this website, by email, by telephone, or while arranging aviation services.

Information we collect and use

We may collect contact details, journey requirements, passenger information, correspondence, and other information you choose to provide. Our website and service providers may also process basic technical information needed to deliver and secure the site.

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, arrange and manage requested services, communicate with clients and suppliers, operate and protect our website, maintain business records, and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

Sharing and retention

Where necessary to provide a requested service, we may share relevant information with aircraft operators, handling agents, transport or catering providers, professional advisers, technology providers, and public authorities. We do not sell personal information.

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including to meet legal, accounting, operational, and dispute-resolution requirements.

Your choices and contact

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the use of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

For privacy questions or requests, contact mark@bespoke-aviation.com. You may also have the right to complain to the data-protection authority responsible for your jurisdiction.
